## About Me

I'm a physicist with experience in mathematical modelling, numerical methods, scientific computing, and quantitative analysis. My research has involved deriving analytical models, implementing Python-based solvers, and evaluating predictions against observational data.

Alongside research, I've pursued software development through independent projects. I've worked with backend services, APIs, databases, streaming data, containers, and deployment workflows. These projects have given me practical experience connecting different software components, as well as a reason to keep exploring how software systems are designed and built.

I'm interested in technical work that combines analytical problem-solving with software implementation—whether in scientific computing, general software development, data systems, simulation, or engineering applications.

## Scientific Background

My research in computational and theoretical physics has focused on gravitational systems, black-hole shadows, and cosmological perturbations.

- **Jadavpur University:** Developed analytical models and Python-based numerical methods for investigating photon trajectories and black-hole shadows, including comparison with published astronomical observations.
- **IIT Bombay (M.Phil.) and IISER Thiruvananthapuram:** Worked on differential equations, perturbation theory, Fourier methods, and mathematical analysis.
- **European Southern Observatory, Germany:** Simulated ALMA radio-interferometric observations with CASA and evaluated the effects of instrument configurations on expected measurement quality.

**Publication:** [*Shadows of a generic class of spherically symmetric, static spacetimes*](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1140/epjp/s13360-024-04993-8) — *The European Physical Journal Plus* (2024).
